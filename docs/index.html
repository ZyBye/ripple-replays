Runs on your GPU

Trim and compress

Share a link
Upload a clip and copy the link without leaving the app.
Lives in the tray

Questions

Is Ripple free?

Yes, and there are no paid features.

Does it send my clips or data anywhere?

No accounts and no analytics. Ripple only checks GitHub for updates (you can turn that off), and uploads a clip to Catbox if you choose to share it.

Where are my clips saved?

In Videos\Ripple by default, or any folder you pick in Settings. Uninstalling Ripple leaves your clips alone.

Which GPUs are supported?

NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel hardware encoders, with a CPU fallback. I don't own an NVIDIA card, so I haven't been able to test that path myself. Reports are welcome.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Open Settings, click Logs, copy them, and paste them into a GitHub issue along with what you were doing.

Get Ripple
